## Key Decisions

- Status register on the rising edge with a bypass into decode — the falling-edge register put the
  forwarding mux, shifter and ALU on a 20 ns path and let ADCS/SBCS see their own carry.
- The delivered word D is the decode-stage register (no separate IF/ID) — 48 flops and a cycle of fetch
  latency saved when the engine took the design to 80.4%.
- One-adder ALU and one-rotator shifter — four adders and three shifters were 240 cells of waste.
- UART cut, meter kept — the section 11 drop order at 72.4%; UART_EN still blanks the video.
- Every abort restarts the stream at the fetch address and drops D — one source of truth for the restart.
- Sampling rule stated from the SCK falling edge, strap 2 nominal — both chips launch data on the
  falling edge (tCLQV); the v0.3 sentence was wrong by half a period; the sweep confirmed the table.
- MRS/MSR and r15 loads and stores decode as NOP; shifter carry and the `#0` shift encodings are out —
  review found MRS decoding as a flag-setting TST; the rest is test surface with no demo value.
- Direct commits on the design repo's `main` — the TinyTapeout CI runs on the pushed `main` and the
  repository has no other contributor.
